Klei Kodesh

Tools for Torah writing, inside Word

What is this?

Klei Kodesh is a free add-in for Word. It adds a Klei Kodesh dropdown to the Home tab, with tools that help you write and edit Torah books and documents.

Each tool opens in a side pane next to your document. You can keep typing while it is open.

The add-in is open source and built by volunteers.

The tools in the menu

  • Kitvei Hakodesh - the book library. More below.
  • Kiwix Library - books and encyclopedias with no internet, through Kiwix.
  • Websites - useful Torah websites, inside Word.
  • Torah Design - tools for laying out seforim.
  • Dicta Nakdan - adds nikkud to your text automatically.
  • Regex Find - advanced find and replace.
  • Duplicate Side Pane - opens another pane just like the one that is open.
  • Settings - adjust the add-in.
  • About - this page.

The big Klei Kodesh button opens the tool you chose as the default. Open the small arrow next to it to pick another tool.

Kitvei Hakodesh

A library for reading and searching books. It works on the Zayit book library and runs right inside Word. If there is no library on your computer yet, the app downloads one.

What is in it

  • Book catalog - browse books by category. Switch between a list view and a tiles view.
  • Search - full-text search across all books. To filter by book or category, add @ in the search bar.
  • HebrewBooks - scanned books from HebrewBooks.org.
  • Open file - open a file or folder from this computer.
  • File search - search documents on this computer.
  • Dictionary - Hebrew and Aramaic word meanings.
  • Calendar - the Hebrew date and daily zmanim, for the city you choose.
  • Hameshiv - a study aid and source finder.
  • Measurements - convert halachic measures.
  • Settings - adjust the program to your preferences.

While you read a book

  • Click a line to see its commentaries. Ctrl+click starts a range of lines.
  • Sync commentaries - the commentaries follow the top line automatically.
  • Related books, Versions, and Scanned editions on HebrewBooks.
  • Split view and tabs, plus Workspaces you can save (F8).
  • Highlights and personal notes on the text.
  • Several ways to copy: with the source, with a quotation, without nikkud, and Copy into Word. There is also Export to Word.
  • Dark mode, fonts and text size, and divine name covering - all in Settings.
  • Report a mistake in the book - send a correction straight from the book.

Some books cannot be exported to Word, at the publisher's request. Reading and printing still work.

In Word's right-click menu

Select text in your document and right-click. You will find these Kitvei Hakodesh commands:

  • Search in Kitvei Hakodesh - searches your selected text across all books.
  • Find Book in Kitvei Hakodesh - searches your text in the catalog, to find a book by its name.
  • Compare with Kitvei Hakodesh - select a paragraph in your document, then select the source in the Kitvei Hakodesh pane. The differences are written into your document as tracked changes. You can accept or reject them one by one.
  • Open Link in Kitvei Hakodesh - on a link to a book, opens that book in the pane.

Dicta Nakdan

Adds nikkud to the selected text, using Dicta's online service. It needs an internet connection.

  • Pick the text type: rabbinic, biblical, modern Hebrew or poetry.
  • You can mark styles for it to skip.
  • Vowelization suggestions - select a word, click Show, and pick another nikkud. Clicking a suggestion replaces the word and moves on to the next one.
  • Clean text - removes nikkud, cantillation marks or punctuation, whichever you choose.

Torah Design

  • Paragraph design - a style for the first word, window design (single and double), and center last line. You choose which paragraphs it applies to.
  • Column balancing - align the columns, and find columns that are not balanced.
  • Spacing - before and after a paragraph, between lines, between words, and letter stretch.

Each action counts as one step in Word, so Ctrl+Z undoes it.

Regex Find

Find and replace in Word with regular expressions (regex). It lets you search for patterns, like "any number" or "a word that starts with a certain letter".

  • Regex character palette - ready-made buttons for characters and symbols. Hover over one to read what it does.
  • You can switch to Word wildcards, or back to Word built-in search.
  • Search direction: all, down, up, or in selection.
  • When you replace, you can also change formatting: font, style, color, bold, italic and more. You can copy formatting from the selection.
  • There is a search history and a replace history. Enter goes to the next result, Shift+Enter to the previous one.

Kiwix Library and Websites

Kiwix Library reads ZIM files. These are books, Wikipedia and other sites saved on your computer, so you can read with no internet.

Websites opens Torah websites in tabs, inside Word. The list includes, for example, the Klei Kodesh site, Dicta, Hameshiv, Otzar HaChochma and HebrewBooks. You can edit the list during installation.

Settings

In Settings you can:

  • Hide buttons you do not need.
  • Choose which tool opens when you click the big button.
  • Choose the add-in language: Hebrew or English.
  • Turn off automatic update checks.
  • Install macros (see below).
  • Reset the settings.

Some settings only take effect after you close Word and open it again.

Macros (optional)

You can also install the add-in's commands as macros. Then you can put them on the Quick Access Toolbar, or give them a keyboard shortcut.

  • In Settings, under "VBA macros", choose: None, Hebrew macro names or English macro names.
  • Close Word and open it again.
  • If the add-in is turned off or missing, a macro shows a clear message, not an error.

Updates

The add-in checks from time to time for a new version and downloads it in the background. You can also check by hand in the Kitvei Hakodesh Settings. If you do not want updates, turn off the automatic check.

It installs only for you, with no administrator rights.
