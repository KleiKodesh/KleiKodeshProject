That exception is the reason these four families are bundled and four other
Culmus families are not. Frank Ruehl CLM, Nachlieli CLM, Miriam Mono CLM and
Drugulin CLM are plain GPL-2.0 with NO exception, so shipping them would put
this application's own source under the GPL. Do not add them here. Verified
2026-08-26 against each font's name table, Debian's per-file copyright, and
Fedora's package licensing.

In the four families bundled here the exception is carried in the name table's
copyright record (nameID 0); their license-description record (nameID 13) is
empty. Taamey Frank CLM carries it in both. Absence from nameID 13 alone is
therefore NOT evidence a Culmus font lacks the exception -- read nameID 0 too.
